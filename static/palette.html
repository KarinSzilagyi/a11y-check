Test your report colours before you build

Paste your colours, sort them into backgrounds, chart colours and text colours, and see which combinations pass WCAG 2.2, also for people with colour vision deficiencies. Then adjust each colour until everything passes.

Add your colours

Paste them, then drag each colour to where it's used in the report. Or click colours to pick them (Shift-click picks a whole run) and move them with the bar that appears. × removes a colour.

Hex codes or a Coolors link land under “Not sorted yet”. A Power BI theme file is sorted for you. To sort them yourself as you paste, put a label in front of each list (a Coolors link works too):

canvas: #F3F2F1
visual: #FFFFFF
chart: coolors.co/118dff-12239e-e66c37
text: #252423, #605E5C

    Fix

    Every colour against every other. Pick a colour in the table and adjust it on the right; the table, scores and preview update as you drag.

    Rows: your chart colours, then your text colours. Columns: the backgrounds, the other chart colours and the text colours. Click any colour in the table to adjust it on the right.

    green: passes red: fails a required check purple: only partly OK (“large only” text, or two chart colours that look alike, Δ under 10) grey: not required, for information Δ colour difference (10 or more is easy to tell apart) ↔ neighbours in series order, which touch in stacked charts
