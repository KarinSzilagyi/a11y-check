Starting the checker

After a quiet spell the checker needs up to 30 seconds to start.

How long does it take? Each page takes 10 to 30 seconds, plus about 30 seconds for every view a bookmark button opens. Most reports are done in 1 to 3 minutes; reports with many buttons take up to 6. This page updates by itself.

What a machine can't judge

    Automated checks show where to look. They can't tell whether alt text describes the insight, whether colour alone carries meaning, or how a screen reader announces your visuals. This is not a WCAG certification.

    What we check

    Five WCAG 2.2 success criteria that can be measured in a published Power BI report, plus an inventory of the visuals you used.

    • Alt text

      WCAG 1.1.1

      Every visual that isn't marked decorative has alt text, so screen reader users know what it shows.

    • Text contrast

      WCAG 1.4.3

      Each text item stands out from what's behind it: 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text.

    • Chart colour contrast

      WCAG 1.4.11

      Bars, lines and slices reach 3:1 against the background and against the colours they touch.

    • Keyboard access

      WCAG 2.1.1

      Every data or interactive visual can be reached with the Tab key.

    • Tab order

      WCAG 2.4.3

      Tab moves left to right, top to bottom, the way people read the page. We draw it on a screenshot of each page.

    • Buttons and tooltips

      All five checks

      We click every bookmark button and check each view it opens, and hover the charts to check report-page tooltips.

    • Visuals used

      Inventory, not scored

      How many core and custom visuals each page uses, which ones, and what each text box, shape, image and button is.

    Find your report link

    The checker needs a public link to the report itself. There are two ways to get one.

    Fabric community entry

    Use the bookmarklet

    The community site blocks automated visitors, so we can't open your entry post. The bookmarklet reads the report link from the post in your own browser.

    1. Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar:
      Check accessibility
    2. Open your entry on community.fabric.microsoft.com.
    3. Click the bookmark. This page opens and starts the check.

    Drag the button to your bookmarks bar; clicking it here does nothing.

    Your own report

    Publish to web

    1. Open the report in the Power BI service.
    2. Choose File › Embed report › Publish to web (public).
    3. Copy the link, not the HTML code, and paste it above.

    Reports that need a sign-in, including links from your workspace, can't be checked.

    Accepted links: app.powerbi.com/view?r=…, app.fabric.microsoft.com/view?r=… and the community report viewer (…/iframeEmbed/…).

    Limits & privacy

    The tool is free, so a few limits keep it fast for everyone.

    • Up to 12 pages per reportLonger reports are checked from the first visible page on; the results say how many were left out.
    • 10 checks per hourPer visitor. Checking the same link again within 10 minutes shows the earlier result.
    • Results are kept for 1 hourBookmark the results page to come back to it. After an hour the results and screenshots are deleted.
    • No sign-in, public reports onlyWe open the report like any visitor would. Nothing is stored beyond the hour.
    • One check at a timeChecks wait in line; the page shows how many are ahead of yours.
    • Button views and tooltips are sampledEach bookmark button is clicked once, for up to 8 views per page and 5 minutes per report. Tooltips are hovered on two data points per chart, so one that only shows for certain data can be missed.
    • Custom visuals are partly visibleTheir text is checked for contrast, but their chart colours often can't be read.
